Enterprise Security

Security that goes beyond a password.

Every layer of BromeoRemote is designed with security first — from the connection to the license.

  • End-to-End Encryption All sessions, chat messages and file transfers are fully encrypted. No one can look in.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is BromeoRemote an alternative to TeamViewer?

Yes. BromeoRemote offers the same core functionality — remote support, viewing, controlling, chatting and transferring files — but with better privacy, HWID security and transparent pricing with no hidden costs.

What is HWID and why is it more secure?

HWID (Hardware ID) binds your license to a unique, irreversible fingerprint of your admin computer. Even if someone got hold of your login details, the license couldn't be used on another PC.

How do I pay and when is my license active?

You pay securely via Mollie (iDEAL, Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay or SEPA). Your license is activated automatically as soon as payment is confirmed — no manual step needed.

Does BromeoRemote work on all Windows versions?

BromeoRemote supports Windows 10 and newer. The Android app requires Android 8.0 or higher. A stable internet connection is required for remote sessions.

💡 How do you activate the license in the BromeoRemote software?

  1. Open the BromeoRemote software on your computer.
  2. Go to the Settings / License page in the menu.
  3. Enter your email address (you@email.com) and your License Key.
  4. Click Check & Activate License. Features and session duration unlock instantly!
